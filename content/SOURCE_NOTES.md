# Source and editorial notes

The card structure and safety language were authored for Emotion Naming Map. The editorial framing is informed by publicly discussed trauma-recovery concepts associated with Pete Walker and Bessel van der Kolk, but the wording and organization here are independently re-expressed.

This project is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official product of either author.

The cards are designed to support naming, reflection, and gentle grounding. They intentionally avoid promises to treat, cure, or prevent PTSD, CPTSD, or any other condition.
